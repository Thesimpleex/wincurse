Method: conditional and hybrid inference on winners (Andrews, Kitagawa and McCloskey, Quarterly Journal of Economics 139(1), 2024). Under normality with a known covariance the conditional interval has exact conditional coverage and the hybrid interval has unconditional coverage of at least the nominal level; with an estimated covariance and non-normal returns both hold asymptotically. Selection is argmax on the same data; discretionary choices, hidden trials and non-stationarity are not covered. Seed 0, 100,000 critical-value draws, generated 2026-10-04.
